BondBoard is a bulletin board for peer-issued fixed-rate bonds on BOT Chain. Post collateral, name your coupon, borrow from the network. No bank. No credit score. Just an on-chain promise and the metal it's backed by.

Three principles *

  1. Credit is a promise between people not a favor from a teller. A bond is that promise, written in code, backed by BOT.
  2. Risk is hidden visible. Every issuer's repaid/defaulted record is stamped on the chain forever no shredder.
  3. Collateral is not a formality — it is the entire recovery process. Under-collateralize at your own peril seriously.
